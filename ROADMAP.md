## Roadmap

Summarized major feature updates, newest first. Upcoming work lives in the milestone checklists in `TODO.md`.

### [v1.0.0] - First Light
- [v0.8.2] **Shatterpoint & stat floors**: Shatterpoint, a new Awakening, makes every critical hit throw out a small burst of ice that breaks the stuns and freezes on whatever it touches for a hit of true damage. Boss Rush now opens with a Lv 90 Echo fight on the way into its Lv 105 round, Aphelion, Blood Pact and Stellar Maelstrom no longer need Master mode, and health, mana, stamina, resistance and knockback resistance can no longer be pushed to nonsense values
- [v0.8.1] **Awakenings in the skill tree**: Hypercarry, Reminiscence, Resonance and Serenade are no longer reward picks. Each is now a 3-point Master-mode keystone on the skill tree. The tree also grows by 14 small nodes (attack speed, crit chance, crit damage, spell damage and a new dash stamina cost reduction), and area of effect is now capped at +200%
- [v0.8.0] **Modes & Contracts**: Contagion, a new Awakening, spreads your DoTs from an enemy you kill to the 3 nearest enemies at half their remaining duration. Shatterpoint, a new status effect, breaks every stun and freeze on a target, and each one it breaks deals a burst of true damage based on your crit damage. Awakenings can now also trigger when an attack's health cost is paid

### [v0.8.0] - Modes & Contracts
- [v0.7.13] **Stampede & boss anomalies**: four new anomalies. Stampede drops the whole wave on you at once, but every enemy has less health, and it can also be taken as a contract. Three new anomalies only appear before boss waves. Twin Crowns spawns a weaker second copy of the boss, and killing one enrages the other. Rampage skips the warm-up: the boss starts in its final phase with less health and enraged. Unstoppable makes the boss immune to knockback, stuns, freezes and pulls, but it takes more damage from everything
- [v0.7.12] **Contract bonus pool retune**: contracts pay out a bonus reward pick much less often, with most anomalies dropping to a 20% chance and Precision and Drought down to 10%. Unlimited Precision now asks for 60-80% accuracy
- [v0.7.11] **Precision & Overcharged**: two more anomalies, both also offered as contracts. Precision asks you to land a set share of your attacks, shows your accuracy live, and fails at wave end if you fall short. Overcharged cuts your attack cooldowns but makes every cast cost more health, stamina and mana
- [v0.7.10] **Hivemind & Drought**: two new anomalies, both also offered as contracts. Hivemind (Expert and up) links every enemy in the wave to one shared health bar, so hitting any enemy drains the pool and they all fall together when it empties. AoE builds shine, single-target builds struggle. Drought stops pickups from spawning and turns off your natural health and stamina regen for the wave
- [v0.7.9] **Contracts**: every run now opens with a contract offer, a run-long take on an anomaly that applies every wave. Each wave you keep its condition, it pays out rerolls, a chance at a skill point and a chance at a bonus reward pick. Break it and you lose only that wave's payout. Contracts can be rerolled, but not on Ironman, and Master won't let you skip them. Holding a contract removes that anomaly type from the anomaly pool for the rest of the run. The Reaper's Spam no longer burns and its Strike gives more warning, and the Frost Slime's Blizzard and Snowstorm slow and freeze less often
- [v0.7.8] **Cult Bat box & balance pass**: Master adds a new spawner box that ambushes you with Cultist Clones and Bats. Pickups are worth more, more of them can be on the map at once, and spawner boxes now favour rerolls and skill points over XP and gold. Cosmic Blaze casts instantly and hits harder, Nebula now has a short cast, and Sacred Surge, Supernova and Hypernova trade raw damage for scaling off Defense. Bat Mark no longer deals damage. Unlimited anomalies hit harder, but Time Trial gives you 120s, and Duel can now show up on boss waves. Defensive milestone rewards now use percent Defense and Arcane Shield, and a dozen milestones were retuned
- [v0.7.7] **Resource refills & spell rebalance**: mana and stamina now fill back up to max at the start of every wave and whenever you level up. Astral Nova, Astral Disjunction and Chaos Theory hit less hard, while Meteor Shower hits harder and casts instantly. Ignition Flash comes off cooldown faster, Subspace Blitz is cheaper on stamina and quicker to reuse, and Nitro Accelerator costs a little less stamina. The Frost Slime's Blizzard and Snowstorm fire fewer projectiles
- [v0.7.6] **Boss attack expansion**: bosses learn new moves. On Expert the Cultist (and its clones) fire a piercing ring of orbs when low on health; on Master the Golem unleashes a bullet-hell barrage from all four sides and the Lich collapses a ring of projectiles inward onto you. The Lich now fights in three phases, the Golem trades some armor for an arcane shield and dodge, and Echo and the Doppelganger hit noticeably softer. Enemy attacks can now pick which animation they play
- [v0.7.5] **Anomaly reroll & skip rules**: Nightmare locks you into the anomalies you're offered, with no rerolling or skipping them, and Master removes the skip. Ironman no longer shows a Reroll button on the anomaly panel, and its reroll counter comes back properly when Ironman is turned off. Blackout now hides pickups outside your vision too
- [v0.7.4] **Mode content gating**: modes now decide much more of the run. Simple is the core loop with only basic pickups and the Swarm and Stat Mod anomalies. Expert turns on corruption, Capstone nodes, spawner boxes, reroll pickups and the Blackout, Duel and Fission anomalies, and teaches enemies new attacks: the Cultist teleports onto a blast, and the Jellyfish, Lich, Bat and Crab bring out extra moves. Master adds the Ethereal Mirage keystone, the rare Cult and Doppelganger boxes, skill point pickups, and a Frost Slime snowstorm. Afflicted (from Bat Mark) lasts longer and hits harder, and Blizzard now deals spell damage
- [v0.7.3] **Expert & Master modes**: a new mode button next to the difficulty selector cycles through Simple, Expert and Master. Simple keeps runs straightforward, with no corruption and no Ultimates; the Ultimate slot stays greyed out for the whole run. Expert unlocks Ultimates, and Master adds corruption, corruption specials and a starting Ultimate, Coherent Strike, a wide piercing slash. Higher modes can also unlock extra attacks, Awakenings and skill nodes, and give enemies new attacks. Spawner box ambushes are smaller across the board. Difficulty no longer decides which enemy attacks appear; the mode does
- [v0.7.2] **Skill tree quality of life**: a search bar filters the tree by name, stat, or the attacks and Awakenings a node grants, outlining matches in cyan and greying out everything else. Nodes you can't afford yet can be queued (shown in blue) and unlock in order as skill points come in, and anything unlocked since the tree was opened can be undone for free until it closes. Two new spawn patterns: lines converging from all four sides, and an inward-collapsing ring
- [v0.7.1] **Nightmare & Blackout**: a fourth difficulty, Nightmare, with much tougher and more numerous enemies, fewer rerolls and weaker rewards, offset by more frequent, better-paying anomalies and stronger corruption. Attacks can now be limited to a minimum difficulty, so Nightmare can give enemies attacks of their own later. Also new: the Blackout anomaly, which shrinks your vision to a small circle and hides enemies outside it while their attacks stay visible, with each kill briefly widening it
- [v0.7.0] **Run Variety & audit fixes**: a full bug audit. Counter-dodge Awakenings finally trigger, health costs no longer set off on-hit effects, negative Defense no longer doubles damage, status effect potency scales every effect, No Hit fails on any hostile damage, summons match their summoner's level, and skill tree undo restores what a capstone replaced. New: the Moonbound Instinct capstone for Starlit Reflexes behind a Maximum Mana chain, and Echo, the Mirror boss, as the final fight of Boss Rush. The skill tree now pans with a plain drag

### [v0.7.0] - Run Variety
- [v0.6.18] **Resonance & Midas Touch**: two new Awakenings. Resonance rewards committing to one attack: cast the same slot three times in a row and the fourth is free, with a stack of Stellar Resonance for extra penetration and projectile speed. Midas Touch turns a hoarded purse into damage, +2% for every 250 gold held. Aeternus, a slow, long-lived Ultimate that scales off almost every stat, comes off hold and joins the Rare Pool from wave 25. The home screen version label is current again
- [v0.6.17] **Sealed & Doppelganger**: a new anomaly seals one of your attack slots for the wave and speeds up the cooldowns of the other two, so the build has to survive a wave without its crutch. The Doppelganger, a smaller cousin of the Mirror boss that fights with your own attacks and Awakenings, joins Unlimited from wave 40. Cooldown buttons now flash green when an attack comes back, turn yellow while an attack is partly restocked, and grey out while sealed
- [v0.6.16] **Mirror boss**: a boss that fights with your own build. When it spawns it copies your current attacks and Awakenings, then uses them against you on its own enemy stats, so the stronger your kit, the harder it hits back. A few Awakenings that only make sense on the player stay behind. Joins the Unlimited boss pool
- [v0.6.15] **Spawner boxes**: an ambush you choose to start. Step on a box and a small group of enemies spawns around it, a couple of levels above the wave; clear them all and a spread of XP, gold, reroll and skill point pickups drops in its place. Four boxes ship (Slime, Bat, Crab and Cultist Clone), and the ambush counts toward the wave, so it has to be finished before the wave ends
- [v0.6.14] **Reward lock**: lock one card on the reward panel and a reroll only replaces the others, so rerolling becomes a decision instead of a slot machine. One lock at a time, released after each reroll, and off in Ironman. The skill tree gains Status Effect Potency and Maximum Stamina paths, and Unlimited synergies can now roll defensive, crit, regen and utility stats
- [v0.6.13] **Sorted cooldown bar and fixes**: attack cooldown buttons are now always ordered Basic, Skill, Ultimate. Momentum finally grants its buff, the Cultist Clone escalates as it loses health, Nocturnis can roll from wave 25, and percent Defense and Arcane Shield join the regular reward pools
- [v0.6.12] **Defense and Arcane Shield**: two new defensive stats that use the armor formula. Defense reduces all damage, and Arcane Shield does for spell damage what armor does for physical damage. Flat damage, physical and spell resistance no longer drop from rewards or gear; they now come only from buffs, debuffs and enemy presets. Enemy arcane shield grows with level like armor
- [v0.6.11] **Stat synergies**: a new permanent reward that turns part of one stat into another, like "Increases Attack by 18% of Max Health". The bonus follows the source stat live for the rest of the run. Synergy offers are not tied to a pool, corruption or a wave interval: from wave 10, each wave has a small chance to follow its reward with a synergy panel, and that chance climbs every wave until one appears. Offers show up in purple-blue
- [v0.6.10] **Attack stacks**: attacks can now store more than one use. Each use spends a stack, and the cooldown restores them one at a time, so you can fire again while it is still recharging. Attacks with more than one stack get a green border on their cooldown button, and the tooltip shows how many stacks are ready
- [v0.6.9] **Performance pass**: projectiles, damage numbers, enemy AI and UI do far less work per frame and per hit. Single-shot attacks spawn instantly, projectiles remember their owner's components, damage numbers draw on their own canvas and allocate less, and idle UI stops redrawing. The Decoy no longer taunts on spawn: enemies go for it only when it is the nearest target on their next retarget. Also fixes a delayed Soul Rend removal that could strip a freshly reapplied stack, and a Mirage clone-death callback that could reach a recycled effect
- [v0.6.8] **Momentum & balance pass**: new Awakening Momentum gives you a burst of move speed and Rush Impact Strength each time you start a dash or rush. Enemy attack and armor now scale more gently with level. The Cultist summons more often and heals faster per summon. Nerfs to Nitro Accelerator (and its Decay self-debuff), Ultrasonic, Warp (Capstone) and Terminal Cascade
- [v0.6.7] **Nitro Accelerator & rush impact**: a new Rush Impact Strength stat makes rush impacts hit and push harder, and rushes can now set off an attack wherever they connect. The first to use it is Nitro Accelerator, a capstone for Subspace Blitz: a faster, longer rush you are immune during, a massive explosion on every impact, and a projectile that stuns, at the price of stacking Decay on yourself each cast. New skill tree nodes for Rush Impact Strength and knockback lead to it. Awakenings can now also trigger on rush impact, just before a teleport, and whenever a Mirage clone is summoned
- [v0.6.6] **Ethereal Mirage & keystones**: the first keystone node, Ethereal Mirage, makes every Ultimate summon 3 translucent clones for 18s (24s cooldown). They follow you in a ring, repeat every attack you cast from their own positions with half your flat stats, and each living clone gives +12% move speed and -15% damage. Clone hits count as yours for Awakenings, lifesteal and rewards. The effect behind it is generic, so enemies can use it too. Enemy targeting was reworked alongside it: enemies now chase whichever player, clone or decoy is nearest, re-checking every 1-3s. Also a small balance pass: Aphelion, Nirvana, Warp Rift, Autopilot and Ultrasonic up; Subspace Blitz, Astral Disjunction and Supersonic down
- [v0.6.5] **Freeze, Shock Absorber & multi-stat scaling**: Freeze is now its own status effect. On top of a stun, frozen targets stop regenerating health and cannot be knocked back, pulled or carried by a rush. Subspace Blitz and the Frost Slime's Blizzard apply it. Rushes that hit an enemy can now deal impact damage as well as knockback, and Subspace Blitz uses it. Attacks can scale off a weighted mix of stats, so Starfury now also scales with Intelligence and Supersonic with attack. New Awakening: Shock Absorber, where each hit you take grants stacking physical damage and move speed at the cost of damage resistance. Enemies no longer get i-frames when hit, the player's hurt i-frames are longer (0.4s), and the Golem's Charge is now a real rush. Also includes a balance pass on a dozen attacks and Awakenings
- [v0.6.4] **Subspace Blitz**: the first attack to use rushes, a rare-pool Skill that throws the player toward the cursor in a short burst and fires a piercing projectile that can freeze enemies for 2s. Supersonic and Ultrasonic now also go off when a rush ends, so the dash Awakenings pair with it
- [v0.6.3] **Combat performance pass**: enemy follow-up attacks (`nextAttack`) now queue and fire on the enemy's next attack check, once the target is in range, instead of running straight after the previous attack. Under the hood, dense fights do much less work. Homing and orbiting projectiles look for a new target at most every 0.15s and skip other projectiles and pickups. Each hit and damage instance reuses cached components instead of looking them up again. Every enemy health bar has its own canvas, so one enemy moving no longer rebuilds every bar. Status effect copies are pooled instead of created and destroyed on each application. Debuff spreading runs at 10 Hz, and HUD cooldowns, damage numbers and anomaly text refresh only when something changes
- [v0.6.2] **Rush attacks & anomaly tooltips**: attacks can now carry their owner with them — a rush toward the cursor (or target), a fixed angle or an offset from it, lasting a set time or distance at a multiple of move speed. Rushes can be steered with a turn-rate cap, grant immunity, hold back other attacks until they end, stop on impact or bounce off walls, knock back enemies they hit, and (for enemies) lead a moving target. Interrupt resistance decides what a stun or knockback does to a rush: end it, pause it, or nothing at all. Two new upgrade triggers fire when a rush starts and ends. Anomaly choices also gained hover tooltips spelling out their rules, time limits and completion rewards. No existing attack uses rushes yet
- [v0.6.1] **Fission & Chaos Theory**: a sixth anomaly splits slain enemies into weaker copies of themselves — 30-50% chance depending on the asset, 2-5 copies spawned around the corpse at 3-5 levels below the original, with every copy added to the wave counter so the wave is not clear until all of them are down. Copies cannot split again, and the anomaly never lands on a boss wave. Also new: the Chaos Theory Awakening, which drops a heavy spell explosion on the player 0.15s after every teleport, applying the new Spellworn debuff (-15% spell resistance, 2 stacks) on hit and stunning 40% of the time
- [v0.6.0] **Environmental collectibles**: pickups now appear around the player while a wave is running — health, XP, stamina, mana, gold, skill points and rerolls, each a `CollectibleData` asset with its own roll chance, spawn cooldown, value range and time on the ground. The four resource types pay a percentage of the matching live stat (`EffMaxHp`, `XpReq`, `EffMaxStamina`, `EffMaxMana`) so they stay relevant at any wave, each pickup shows what it will pay before you take it and pops a descriptive number when you do, and a pickup left behind at wave end keeps its remaining time into the next wave instead of vanishing. Reroll pickups never spawn in Ironman Mode. Also: the default dash is stronger and far cheaper — speed multiplier 4 → 5, cooldown 4s → 2s, stamina cost 35 → 20

### [v0.6.0] - Impact and Efficiency
- [v0.5.7] **Cheaper hits, bars and barrages**: upgrades are now looked up by the trigger that fired instead of scanning every upgrade the player owns, so a swarm hit costs a fraction of what it did with a full build; health regeneration only reads its stats twice a second and health bars only move when the entity or the camera actually moved; and projectile barrages stopped allocating a timer object per shot. Also fixed: a player upgrade with a delay set on a spawn-position trigger no longer loses that position. No gameplay changes
- [v0.5.6] **Ultrasonic, `OnTeleport` & dash nodes**: upgrades can now trigger when the player teleports through a `teleportToProjectile` attack, and the new Ultrasonic capstone uses it. It replaces Supersonic with a faster, harder-hitting burst of 7 stunning projectiles that fires on every dash end and every teleport with no cooldown. Two new 3-node chains (Dash Cooldown Reduction and Dash Distance) lead to it. Supersonic hits harder but lost its slow, and DoTSpread ships as the treasure-pool Wipeout upgrade, which spreads the player's debuffs to nearby enemies
- [v0.5.5_1] **Enemy charges sustain again & auto-pause**: enemy charge attacks open the charge window like the player does, so their sustained projectiles stay alive for the whole charge instead of expiring at their authored lifetime, and the pause menu opens by itself when the game window loses focus
- [v0.5.5] **Teleport to projectile & Astral Disjunction**: attacks can teleport their caster to the first projectile they spawn (`teleportToProjectile` + `teleportDelay`), and the new Astral Disjunction capstone upgrades Astral Nova into a longer-range blink nuke
- [v0.5.4_1] **Cheaper status effects**: `StatusEffectManager` reads effect resistance once per frame instead of once per effect, sits out paused (zero-timescale) frames, and only re-checks its effect list after callbacks that can change it, applies effects without allocating a closure or string-comparing when the same effect is re-applied, so swarms carrying burns and slows cost less every frame
- [v0.5.4] **Leaner combat hot path**: big swarm fights stop feeding the garbage collector on every hit. Damage numbers, hurt animations, i-frames and projectile re-hit windows now run on timers instead of starting a coroutine per hit, damage packets are reused from a pool (CrystalFlux Core 0.10.0), and projectiles reuse one on-hit effect list instead of allocating a new array each hit. Nothing about how combat plays should change
- [v0.5.3_1] **Impact feedback tuned & the run extended**: hit stop / screen shake values are now authored across ten player attacks (Cyclone Cleave, Cosmic Blaze, Exodus, Luminaria, Nebula, Nirvana, Nocturnis, Revelation, Shattered Singularity), the wave chain grew a fifth sequence (`ws_5`, waves 61-75 ending on a Lv 75 Grim Reaper), Boss Rush levels were raised from 70 to 85 with the Reaper added as a fifth boss, and the Golem's Orbit no longer roots it in place (`castTime` 0)
- [v0.5.3] **Hit stop & screen shake**: every `AttackData` can now freeze the game for a moment on hit (`hitStop`, with a `hitStopCooldown` so rapid hits don't lock the game up) and kick the camera (`screenShake`), both off by default so impact is authored attack by attack — an ult can land with a real freeze while basics stay smooth. Shake runs through a Cinemachine impulse that `CombatFeedback` wires up on its own, and the player's camera also shakes when they get hit. Freezes step aside for the pause menu, skill tree and reward panel instead of fighting them over `timeScale`
- [v0.5.2] **Quit & build version**: the pause menu and death screen gain a Quit button that saves settings and exits cleanly through `GameRestart.QuitGame`, and the home screen now shows the build version (`BuildVersionLabel`, reading `Application.version`) so playtest reports name the build. The label hides itself when the run starts via `DifficultySelector.hideOnLockIn`
- [v0.5.1] **The Grim Reaper (in progress)**: a fifth boss built on the *Bringer of Death* sprite sheet, with attacks designed by Fred Xu — Toss (stuns) above 60% HP, Strike (Vulnerable) above 30% HP, Balls from phase 2, and Spam, a slow wall of long-lived burning projectiles. It only spawns from the `test` wave for now. Also: difficulty assets moved to `Assets/data/Difficulty/`, the Cultist's Ball gained range and unlocks a phase earlier, and Sacred Surge and Hypernova were toned down

### [v0.5.0] - Feel and Foundations
- [v0.4.10] **Shared attack data**: attacks and projectile data are no longer deep-cloned per entity, per summon and per equip — every handler reads the authored `AttackData` / `ProjectileData` asset directly, and all of their serialized fields became `[SerializeField] private` behind read-only properties so nothing can write to shared state by accident. The whole runtime-copy stack went with it: the recursive deep clone, the live-data reference counting in `Projectile`, the deferred-destroy queue in `PlayerAttackHandler`, and the summon-side re-instantiation. Per-run additions now register on the owner instead, through the new `IAttackEffectSource` — Soul Rend attaches its buff to the Basic and Skill *slots* rather than to two attack objects, so it follows an attack swap and is removed with the upgrade. Also fixed: the Warp capstone's projectiles pointed at the base Warp's attack asset, paying out its resource gains instead of their own
- [v0.4.9_2] **Death screen & abbreviated bars**: dying now stops the run on a *You Died* panel with a restart button, instead of leaving a dead player standing in the wave — it pauses through `MenuPause`, closes every settings page behind it, blocks `Escape` from opening the pause menu over it, and hands off to the existing `GameRestart` flow. The new `NumberFormat` static abbreviates every health, mana, stamina and XP readout to `k`/`M`, and the player health label now shows its overhealth pool explicitly the way enemy bars already did
- [v0.4.9] **Restart run & Oblivion**: a Restart button on the pause page, behind a confirmation panel, reloads the scene back to the home screen — `GameRestart` resets the pause depth and time scale on both sides of the load, and `Projectile`'s statics now clear on scene unload so nothing carries into the next run. Oblivion joins as an Exsanguinate capstone (100% conversion, 15% decay), and 13 stat nodes land alongside it (196 to 210) — a closed 6-node Dodge Chance ring, a third Dodge Resistance node, and Status Effect Duration and Tick Rate chains off Spell Resistance. Rebalances: Exsanguinate decays twice as fast, Terminal Cascade retriggers 20% of the time, and Swarm's description finally reports the spawn increase it actually applies
- [v0.4.8_1] **Resistances & stat breadth**: four pairs of new skill tree nodes (188 to 196) — Dodge Resistance, Spell Resistance, Maximum Stamina and Projectile Speed — each hanging off an existing stat branch and tying into the outer Damage ring. The Jellyfish joins the phase-buff club: faster below 70% HP, and below 50% it spreads its attacks and regenerates. Rebalances: Hypernova's cooldown and damage split (physical → physical + spell), a trimmed Celestial Protection, Duel enemies that now gain armor too, and the Unlimited anomaly trio (Duel roll max 600, Swarm 20-60, Time Trial 90s)
- [v0.4.8] **Swarm & Duel anomalies**: two new wave modifiers with no failure condition — Swarm weakens every enemy by the rolled percentage and spawns twice that percentage more of them, Duel puts a single heavily buffed enemy on the wave with its own boss bar and status effect display. Anomaly assets gained `disallowOnBossWave` (so neither can land on a boss wave), moved out of the scripts tree into `Assets/data/Wave/Anomaly/`, and enemy-side buffs now go through an `ApplyEnemyBuffs` hook instead of a `StatModifierInstance` type test. Enemies gained an authored `displayName`, which the boss bar now prefers over the prefab name
- [v0.4.7] **Refund All & the outer ring**: one button undoes the entire skill tree for the summed gold cost, returning every spent point and clearing the starting choice — hidden in Ironman Mode. Ships with 41 nodes (148 to 188): a 24-node Damage I ring around the outside of the tree, plus attack speed, cast speed, healing and knockback resistance branches that tie the existing attack, intelligence, health and armor trees into it
- [v0.4.6] **Hypernova**: a Supernova capstone that upgrades the attack in place — bigger, higher pierce, and it now pulls enemies in, self-buffs with Celestial Protection and can stun. Ships with 9 supporting nodes (137 to 147) across a new added-physical-damage chain and two Defense III branches that tie the armor and regen trees together. `Escape` now closes the skill tree instead of opening the settings menu. Rebalances: Blaze family, Cyclone Cleave, Sacred Surge, Exsanguinate, enemy attack scaling, unlimited-wave enemy counts
- [v0.4.5] **Golem**: the fourth boss — a slow, heavily armoured bruiser whose moveset unlocks with its `EnemyPhase` stages: Slam and Cross from the start, Charge below 80% HP, and a self-orbiting stone barrage below 40%. Its Charge is `selfApply`, stunning the Golem for 3s and sometimes buffing its attack by 40%. Ships with the `ws_4` wave sequence (waves 46-60, Golem at 60) and a slot in the Unlimited boss rotation. Enemy assets were also split into `Bosses/` and `Enemies/`, and `WaveManager`'s rarity table was resynced with `UnlimitedWaveManager`'s
- [v0.4.4] **Ironman Mode**: an opt-in home-screen toggle that removes every take-back from a run — rerolls are forced to 0 and their button hidden, corruption is unavailable, and skill node refunds are refused. It persists to `settings.json` (`ironmanMode`) and locks in when a gamemode button is pressed, the same way the difficulty selector does. Outside Ironman, starting skill nodes became refundable and re-pickable. Also fixed: orbit interactions and the `OnCast` summon roll no longer fire on press before the tap/hold split is known
- [v0.4.3] **Difficulty selector**: a home-screen cycler that picks Easy / Normal / Hard before the run starts, persisted to `settings.json` and locked in when a gamemode button is pressed. Each difficulty is a `DifficultyData` asset holding additive offsets for enemy scaling, reward count and quality, occasional wave rewards, anomalies, corruption and economy, read at the point of use so Normal is byte-for-byte the old behaviour. Easy also grants pre-run free picks — a reward panel of rare/treasure choices before wave 1, reusing the existing reward flow via the new `RewardType.PreRun`
- [v0.4.2_2] **Sacred Surge & input buffering**: a new armor-scaling rare-pool Skill that cleanses debuffs and can be held to sustain its field; attacks pressed during a cast or charge are now queued and fired on release (`maxQueuedAttacks`, `queueExpiry`); floating numbers abbreviate to k/M and moved to a `TextType` enum; `enableExtraSpawns` lets unlimited waves spawn one enemy per tick. Rebalances: Supernova, unlimited-wave rarity weights
- [v0.4.2] **Overhealth & attack chains**: overhealth — health held above `EffMaxHp`, spent before health and decaying over time — plus the `overhealth` and `healingPct` stats (Core v0.9.0). Four new treasure-pool upgrades: Exsanguinate, Terminal Cascade, Cresendo and Tempo.
- [v0.4.1] **Settings menu**: an `Escape`-opened pause panel with live gameplay toggles and full keyboard rebinding, persisted to `settings.json` by the new `CrystalFlux.Settings` assembly.
- [v0.4.0] **Object pooling Expansion**: one shared static `PrefabPool` (prefab-keyed, capped, with an opt-in `IPoolable` reset hook) now backs health bars, status effect icons, cast bars, damage numbers, reward/anomaly buttons and projectiles; `Projectile.Start` became `Setup`. Surfaced eight latent reuse bugs, including per-frame health-bar rebuilds and an unbounded damage-number pool. Enemies remain unpooled

### [v0.4.0] - Content Updates
- [v0.3.13] **Charged attacks**: hold-to-charge attacks via the `AttackData` charging block (`canCharge`, `chargeThreshold`, `minChargeTime`/`maxChargeTime`, `chargeTickInterval`, `chargeAttack`), sustained by ticked projectile lifetimes and the new `IChargeRegister`; plus `MovementType.FollowCursor`, the `corruptionSpecialPool` corruption outcome, and the chargeable Ultimate Nocturnis. Rebalances: Blood Pact, Lifeforce Burst, level-up rewards, rare pool unlock waves.
